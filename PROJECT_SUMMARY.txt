## 🔄 UPDATE WORKFLOW

### To Update Videos
1. Open `/data/videos.json`
2. Add/modify YouTube video entries
3. Changes appear automatically on all pages

### To Update Products
1. Open `/data/impianti.json`
2. Add/modify product entries in sections
3. Changes appear automatically on all pages
4. Images must be in `/assets_nuovi/`

### To Add New Images
1. Place image in appropriate subfolder of `/assets_nuovi/`
2. Add entry to impianti.json with correct path
3. Changes appear immediately
