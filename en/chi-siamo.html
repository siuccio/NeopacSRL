Industrial solutions designed to last

Since 1993, Neopac Srl, under the guidance of founder Claudio Crucitti, designs and manufactures micro-dosing, dosing, counting, and vertical packaging systems. In a market where many producers compete on extreme speed, Neopac chose a different path: maximum precision, robustness, reliability, and durability over time. Our machines are designed to work intensively, minimizing downtime and guaranteeing high productivity over time, without resorting to record-breaking speeds. The goal is to offer solid solutions, integrated into real production lines, with an excellent performance-to-cost ratio. Every project is born to be unique, built on the client's real needs: • Ease of use • Custom-designed components • Qualified installation and technical support • Spare parts warehouse always available • Tailored solutions for every production need Our mission is to guarantee customers not just a machine, but a reliable partner, capable of supporting the growth and efficiency of their production over time.
